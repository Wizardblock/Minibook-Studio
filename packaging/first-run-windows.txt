MiniBook Studio {version} for Windows

1. Unzip this folder anywhere (it does not need installing).
2. Double-click MiniBookStudio.exe.
   Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app is not signed yet.
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
3. To try it, drop samples\sample_library.csv onto the Books tab.

Your settings are kept in your user profile, so deleting this folder removes the app but not your choices.
Project page: https://github.com/Wizardblock/Minibook-Studio
